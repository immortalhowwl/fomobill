How the list and consent are stored
Explicit consent saves an address on the server for 7 days. It does not connect a wallet or prove ownership. Bookmarks are separate. Clearing cookies removes access to this list; monitoring expires automatically.




Collection limits and supported trades
Pump bonding-curve buy/sell, finalized. Bounded sample of the exact account, excluding ATAs and AMMs. Up to 8 addresses per browser; 32 per service, 6 RPC/min, 400 transactions. Groups: ≥2 participants, 300-second window. Delays and gaps are possible.

Source coverage and version limitations
  • DexScreener: up to 12 Solana pairs where the entered address is the base token, sorted by provider-reported USD liquidity. Not all markets or confirmation of token safety.
  • Solana RPC: one getSignaturesForAddress request, at most 20 records, commitment finalized. Includes references to the exact address, not only actions by its owner. Associated token accounts are not queried separately. This one-time lookup does not decode trades. The separate monitor decodes supported Pump bonding-curve events.
  • An empty response and an unavailable source are distinct. Unknown prices, times or links remain unknown. Snapshot retrieval time is not block time.
  • Monitor: at most 8 addresses per browser, 32 per service, 6 RPC requests per minute, 400 saved transactions; new addresses start with 5 signatures. Groups are purchases by different monitored participants within 300 seconds, not proof of coordination. A shared SOL transfer source does not prove common ownership. A sale does not mean a complete exit. No AMMs, complete history, positions, PnL, X reading or AI.
  • A saved address is a bookmark, not an event subscription. No copy trading, wallet connections, transaction signing, paid APIs or model requests.
