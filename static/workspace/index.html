How monitoring works
The server checks a bounded sample of supported Pump bonding-curve trades. Two or more watched wallets buying the same token within five minutes form a group. Subsequent observed sales are shown with their transactions.
Coverage is incomplete. A sale does not prove a full exit. No sale found does not mean still holding. Monitoring expires after 7 days unless renewed.
This browser’s cookie gives access to the private list. Clearing it loses access; monitoring still expires automatically.

KNOW WHAT YOU’RE SEEING
The data, explained.
Token holders
An available account sample, not every holder or a global ranking. Source time may be unknown.
Holder trades
Small pages of supported decoded events. Coverage can include Pump, PumpSwap and selected Raydium / Orca fills. It is not complete history or net route proceeds.
Wallet monitoring
A bounded sample of supported Pump bonding-curve trades. Two watched wallets buying the same token within five minutes can form a group. This is not proof of coordination.
Manual lookup
Up to 20 finalized activity records or 12 DexScreener market pairs. Activity records are not decoded buy/sell history.
No sale found ≠ still holding.
Some activity may be missing. A sale is not necessarily a complete exit. Balances, profit and loss are not inferred.
